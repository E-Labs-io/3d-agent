Print notes
  • Nozzle 1.2 / 1.4mm — outer walls 3.6mm (3 perimeters), dividers 2.4mm (2 perimeters), floor 2.4mm.
  • Build volume 300³ — each tile (199 × 204 × 62mm) fits with margin.
  • Clearance 0.5mm all-round to drawer wall · lid clearance 0.3mm.
  • Tile seam: 1.8mm tongue on Tile A + matching pocket on Tile B. No bolts unless you want them added.
  • Print orientation: floor-down. No supports needed if dividers stay vertical.
  • Material: PETG recommended for road-case transit (impact-tolerant) · PLA acceptable if storage stays cool.
