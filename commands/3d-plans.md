---
description: Generate projected 2D drawings (SVG and DXF) for the current project
argument-hint: "[project slug]"
---

1. Project: "$ARGUMENTS", or the one shown in the viewer.
2. `cad_build(project, show=False)` then `drawing_make(project)`.
3. List the sheets with scale and overall sizes, and link each SVG. They also appear in the viewer's Docs panel.
